World Fight: Election Edition

Play now – free, in your browser

World Fight: Election Edition is a console-style satirical fighting game that caricatures Israeli politics ahead of the 26th Knesset. Pick a fighter, enter the arena and win a best-of-three match. It is free, has no ads and needs no download: it runs directly in the browser on a phone or a computer. The in-game text is in Hebrew with English labels.

This game is satire only. Any resemblance between the characters and reality is purely coincidental, and it contains no call for or encouragement of violence in the real world. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any person, party or organisation shown.

Fighters

13 parody fighters: Bennet, Lieberman, Netanyahu ("Bibi"), Yair Golan, Aryeh Deri, Yair Lapid, Mansour Abbas, Benny Gantz, Itamar Ben-Gvir, Bezalel Smotrich, Gadi Eisenkot, Donald Trump and the Joint List. Each has its own look, pace and finishing move.

Modes

  • Quick Fight – choose a fighter and face a random rival.
  • Campaign – four fights against the rest of the roster, with Bibi always last.
  • Difficulty – Easy, Normal, Hard and Expert.
  • Six arenas, including the Knesset exterior, the plenum and Kaplan Junction.

On a phone, rotate to landscape: a joystick on the left and JAB, CROSS, KICK, SP and GUARD buttons on the right.

Special Energy and finishers

The gold bar under each health bar is Special Energy. Clean hits charge it. At 100% you can trigger an authored finisher that takes 30% of the rival's maximum health. Winning the whole match earns a victory celebration.

FAQ
